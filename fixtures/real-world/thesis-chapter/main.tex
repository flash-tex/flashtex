\chapter{Robust Estimation of Recurrence Intervals in Sparse Sensor Networks}
\label{ch:robust}

This chapter develops and evaluates a method for estimating the recurrence
interval of infrequent events observed by a sparse network of low-power
sensors. Section~\ref{sec:background} reviews the relevant background on
point-process estimation and motivates the specific estimator used
throughout the remainder of the thesis, and Section~\ref{sec:method}
presents the method itself, together with its statistical properties and an
empirical evaluation on synthetic and field-collected data.
