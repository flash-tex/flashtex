\section{Thrushes}
\label{sec:thrush}
The song thrush repeats each phrase three times; the mistle thrush prefers the tallest tree. Both are covered again in the comparison of Section~\ref{sec:compare} on page~\pageref{sec:compare}. Nests are open cups of mud and grass, built low in dense cover early in the season.
\section{Finches}
\label{sec:finch}
Chaffinches flash white wing bars in flight, while greenfinches wheeze from the hedgerow. Flock sizes peak in January; see the seasonal table in Section~\ref{sec:compare}. Feeders with black sunflower seed draw both species within days.
\section{Comparing the two}
\label{sec:compare}
Thrushes (Section~\ref{sec:thrush}) sing while finches (Section~\ref{sec:finch}) flock: song marks territory, whereas flocking marks food. The Quick reference below collects the marks; the groups share these pages because the two-column table of contents above pushed them here between passes.
