\section{Back matter, twice over}
\label{sec:backmatter}

A scholarly document ends with two finding aids. The
\index{bibliography}bibliography lists cited sources, while the
\index{index}index lists discussed ideas. Both are generated on a later
pass: the bibliography from a \index{BibTeX}BibTeX run over the
\index{database}database~\cite{plass81,knuth84}, and the index from a
\index{makeindex}makeindex run over the marked terms. Knuth's
handbook~\cite{knuth84} and the incremental account
in~\cite{adapton} cover both. Each marked term points forward to its page
list in the index on page~\pageref{ch:index}.
