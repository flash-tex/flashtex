\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The teaching laboratory keeps a stock of reference springs whose stiffness
was measured during the inventory of Section~\ref{sec:inventory} on
page~\pageref{sec:inventory}. The full catalog is Table~\ref{tab:springs},
a multipage \textsf{longtable} whose column widths are written to the
\texttt{.aux} file on the first pass and read back on the second, so the
header row is misaligned with the body columns on pass~1 and snaps into
alignment on pass~2. The notes on
temperature dependence in Section~\ref{sec:notes} apply to every entry.

\section{Inventory}
\label{sec:inventory}

pairs. Uncertainties are one standard deviation.

\section{Notes}
\label{sec:notes}

The stiffness values in Table~\ref{tab:springs} drift by roughly
$2 \times 10^{-4}$ per $^{\circ}$C; experiments outside $18$--$24^{\circ}$C
should apply the correction posted next to the inventory of
Section~\ref{sec:inventory}.
