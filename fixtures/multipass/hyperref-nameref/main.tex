\chapter{Deployment}
\label{ch:deployment}

In June we deployed twelve low-cost temperature loggers across the north
orchard in a regular grid. This report documents the deployment, the
calibration of \autoref{sec:calibration}, and the corrected readings
analyzed in \nameref{sec:results}. Readers interested only in the outcome
may jump directly to \hyperref[sec:results]{the results section}, whose
headline figure is \autoref{fig:bias} on page~\pageref{fig:bias}.

Each logger records the ambient temperature every ten minutes with a
quoted accuracy of $\pm 0.5^{\circ}$C; bench tests showed offsets of up to
$1.2^{\circ}$C, motivating the calibration of \autoref{sec:calibration}.
Positions and coefficients are in Table~\ref{tab:sites}.

\chapter{Calibration and Results}
\label{ch:main}

\section{Calibration}
\label{sec:calibration}

Every logger was calibrated against a reference thermometer at
$0^{\circ}$C and $35^{\circ}$C by fitting the affine model
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:calib}
  c = a\, r + b,
\end{equation}
which cut the residual bias below $0.1^{\circ}$C (coefficients below).

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

After correction, the loggers agree with the reference station to within
$0.15^{\circ}$C over the full month (Figure~\ref{fig:bias}), confirming that
the affine model \eqref{eq:calib} captures the dominant sensor error.
