\chapter{Breaking and shipping}
\label{ch:main}

\section{Building pages}
\label{sec:pages}

The page builder accumulates a vertical list of
\glossentry{boxes}{units of typeset material with a fixed size} joined by
\glossentry{glue}{flexible space with a natural size plus stretch and
shrink}. When the list holds more than a page, the builder picks a
breakpoint and ships the rest back.

\section{Choosing breaks}
\label{sec:breaks}

Each candidate breakpoint carries a \glossentry{penalty}{a numeric cost
for breaking at a given point, possibly infinite}. The builder totals the
\glossentry{badness}{how far a line or page strays from its ideal size} of
each line with the penalties, then keeps the cheapest path through the
paragraph introduced in Section~\ref{sec:pages} on
page~\pageref{sec:pages}.
