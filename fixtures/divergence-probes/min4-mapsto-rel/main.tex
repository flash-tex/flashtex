Triangle inequality $|u + v| \le |u| + |v|$, and also $\bigl| |u| - |v| \bigr| \le |u - v|$, so that $x \mapsto |x|$ is $1$-Lipschitz and in particular continuous on $\mathbb{R}^n$.
