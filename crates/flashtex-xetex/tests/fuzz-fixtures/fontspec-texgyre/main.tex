A paragraph is broken into lines by choosing the breakpoints that minimise the total
demerits of the paragraph. The badness of a line measures how far its glue stretches or
shrinks from its natural width, and the fonts' own spacing --- here Pagella's --- sets
how much it may. Ligatures such as fi, fl, ffi and ffl come from the font's tables, not
from the format, so a font change changes them too.\footnote{TeX Gyre Pagella is
based on URW Palladio; its oldstyle figures are 0123456789.}

\section{Features}\label{sec:features}
OpenType features are switched on and off by fontspec's options: \texttt{Numbers=OldStyle}
gives the figures 0123456789 here, {\lining and Lining gives 0123456789 there}. Small
capitals come from the \texttt{smcp} feature: \textsc{The Quick Brown Fox}. Letter
spacing, word spacing and scaling are fontspec's own, and colour is a feature as well.
