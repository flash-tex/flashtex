## Honest limits

- Word alignment is by normalised text (difflib); a page where the producer typesets different text (unsupported environments, dropped math) aligns fewer words and the deltas of the aligned ones can be dominated by that upstream difference. `aligned` and the unaligned counts say how much of the page the geometry numbers cover.
- Reference positions are decoded from the content stream with the fonts' /Widths; the text used for alignment maps OT1/T1 ligature slots and /Differences names, everything else becomes `?` and is dropped from the alignment key. Math words in the reference are per-glyph runs and rarely align.
- The rasterizer anti-aliases; differing-pixel counts on a page whose text differs are large by construction and are only the secondary ranking key.
- Owners are heuristic pointers; the crate named for a delta caused by an upstream diagnostic is the diagnostic's owner, not the crate that positioned the word.
