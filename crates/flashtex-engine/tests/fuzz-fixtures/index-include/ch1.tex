\chapter{Paragraphs}\label{ch:par}
A paragraph\index{paragraph} is broken into lines\index{line breaking} by
choosing the breakpoints\index{breakpoint} that minimise the total demerits
of the paragraph. The badness\index{badness} of a line measures how far its
glue\index{glue} stretches or shrinks from its natural width.

Hyphenation\index{hyphenation} adds breakpoints inside words; a discretionary
break\index{discretionary} carries the text before and after the break and the
text with no break. Chapter~\ref{ch:pages} takes the lines further.

Penalties\index{penalty} make a break more or less attractive; an infinite
penalty forbids it, and a negative infinite one forces it.
