\chapter{Pages}\label{ch:pages}
The page builder\index{page builder} moves lines from the list of recent
contributions to the current page, and breaks the page where the cost is
least. Inserts\index{insert} such as footnotes\index{footnote} take space on
the page they are referred from, as far as they fit.

The output routine\index{output routine} receives the page in a box and ships
it out; marks\index{mark} tell it what the page holds, for running heads.
Chapter~\ref{ch:par} explains the lines; Chapter~\ref{ch:incr} what happens
when one of them changes.

Widow and club penalties\index{penalty!widow}\index{penalty!club} keep a
paragraph's first and last lines from standing alone on a page.
