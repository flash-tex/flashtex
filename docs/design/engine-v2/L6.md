# L6: the raw-speed program, planned for P6

Lane **L6** (kabir-claude-l6 under Commander kabir-claude, 2026-11-01). DESIGN.md §5.6 defines L6.
§12 gives P6's gate: measured wins, with P-T1 unchanged. DESIGN.md is the source of truth: where
this plan and DESIGN.md disagree, DESIGN.md wins and this plan is wrong.

## 1. What L6 is, and what it is not

L6 is §5.6's seven items:
1. Profile first. Every item needs a measured win.
2. Contiguous token lists, and argument matching without allocation.
3. A fast `\csname` lookup.
4. Guarded intrinsics (D9), and their two extensions: MACRO-REPLAY (macros with arguments, #1509)
   and BOX-MEMO (measure and draw calls).
5. NEON input scanning, only if the profile shows scanning matters.
6. The per-page output routine, as the first intrinsics target.
7. Pipeline and export parallelism, with identical output only.

**MACRO-REPLAY is not L6.** It is item 4's extension: one candidate inside the program. §13
(2026-10-05) confirms it as the P6 direction, to be implemented only after an ablation and a
real-document measurement show a win. E3 (§3) is that measurement.

**L1–L5 are built**, and P4 was met on 2026-10-21 (#1876):
- **L1:** the resident host, S₀, stored pages and the one-pass reopen;
- **L2:** b3 checkpoints with held segments (#1614);
- **L3:** on-demand structural convergence with line shifting (#1570). PDF object relocation is
  not built;
- **L4:** viewport first;
- **L5:** `.aux` read-sets (#1242), the external tools orchestrated (#1296, #1308), and makeindex
  and bibtex ported (#1706, #1723);
- **§5.7, the segment memo:** not built (it is gated at ≥ 2×).

Everything L6 does must leave L1–L5's outputs byte-identical to a scratch run.

## 3. What L6 changes: bounded experiments, one PR each

- **E0: profile and tool (no product change).** `tools/incr-bench/l6ab.py` and `l6-ab.yml`, on
  the NixOS runner (the PC's ssh needs a Tailscale re-auth):
  - cold compiles, interleaved, under `perf stat`, with PDF identity checked on every run;
  - a `perf` function profile;
  - the BOX-MEMO cold-pass ablation of E3.
- **E1: PGO in the shipped macOS host.** First re-measure on main, PGO against the default build
  of the same SHA, on cold rows and keystroke rows. Train on host keystrokes as well as on
  command-line compiles. Then wire the build into `release.yml`, either by training in the job
  or from a profile pinned by its sha256 (needs the Commander's approval: it changes the
  release).
- **E2: a fast `\csname` lookup (item 3).** A direct-mapped index from a name's fingerprint to
  its `eqtb` location, in front of `id_lookup`'s chain walk (`changes/throughput.ch`). LaTeX's
  format holds tens of thousands of names in 8,501 buckets, so a lookup walks several entries,
  and each one costs dependent loads.
- **E3: MACRO-REPLAY's gate.**
  - **What to measure.** On real beamer decks and a real book: the share of a cold compile that
    expansion-only calls with arguments take (`\XC@col@rlet` was 47 % of a deck on 10-04), and
    how much of it BOX-MEMO's existing machinery replays when it records in cold passes
    (`FLASHTEX_BOXMEMO=on` on the command line records anywhere).
  - **If BOX-MEMO captures the win:** the change is a name added to BOX-MEMO's `RECORD_ANYWHERE`,
    not new MACRO-REPLAY code. That touches BOX-MEMO's recording rules, so the Commander and the
    BOX-MEMO lanes decide first.
  - **If it does not:** MACRO-REPLAY's §9 prototype plan stands, with that measurement as its
    basis.
  - **Measured (2026-11-01, `docs/evidence/l6-2026-11-01/`).** BOX-MEMO captures the win where
    there is one:
    - −31 % instructions on beamer-30 (95 % hits) and −12 % on the beamer-overlays fixture;
    - but +5.3 % on *Infinite Descent* and +12 % on the beamer-blocks-columns fixture, where
      colours are defined again between calls and the hits fall to 37–44 %.

    So the gate is not met as things stand. MACRO-REPLAY's guard would miss on the same
    redefinitions, so nothing here argues for its separate code.
  - **With a recording budget (the Commander's go).** The name was put in `RECORD_ANYWHERE`,
    stopped after N offers below a hit rate. It does not land: no threshold keeps beamer-30's
    win and holds every other document within +0.5 %.
    - Early hit rates are the same, about 35 % over the first 1,024 offers, on the decks that
      win and those that lose.
    - A recording costs about 0.7 M instructions against 0.25 M saved per hit.

    What would make it pay is a cheaper recording: BOX-MEMO's machinery, or MACRO-REPLAY's §9.
    The branch `agent/kabir-claude-l6/xc-anywhere` stays as a record.
- **E5 (later): export parallelism.** Only for exports and the command line, using the 09-29
  writer-thread sketch.

**Bounds.** Each experiment stops after two serious attempts that fail its bar (§5), and the
lane reports that rather than escalating.

## 4. Soundness argument

There are three kinds of change, each with its own argument:
- **(a) Build-level (E1).** The source is the same, and the compiler transforms safe Rust in ways
  that preserve its meaning. Bounds checks stay. The remaining risk is a compiler bug, so the
  gates run on the PGO binary itself: P-T1/P-T2, lockstep, trip and etrip, and the sweeps.
- **(b) An equal routine (E2, `throughput.ch`'s kind).** The routine computes the same result in
  every state, as the change file's comment proves and a test against the original routine
  checks. State kept outside the word space is only a cache, and every use validates it against
  the word space. So restores, convergence jumps, format images and S₀ loads cannot make it
  answer wrongly, and checkpoints and `iso.rs` never see it.
- **(c) Guarded replay (E3).** MACRO-REPLAY.md's rule and gates (a)–(g), or BOX-MEMO's, apply
  unchanged, with the verify mode's full-state diff.

**E2 in detail.**
- **What the walk returns.** `id_lookup` (§259) returns the entry with text equal to the name in
  the chain of bucket h(name), or inserts one after a failed search.
- **Where texts come from.** A position in the hash proper (`hash_base` to
  `hash_base+hash_size`, plus the `hash_extra` range) gets its text only from that insertion, and
  never loses it. An undump or a restore installs a state that was made the same way.
- **So a name has at most one entry**, and that entry is in its bucket's chain.
- **What a hit must prove.** An index hit is used only when its location is such a position and
  its text in the current state equals the name byte for byte. That location is then the one the
  walk would return. Otherwise the walk runs unchanged.
- **Positions the walk never returns.** The frozen and font-identifier positions, whose texts
  repeat real names, are never returned by the walk, so they are never stored in the index.
- **The hooks.** The hooks after `found` (read-sets, BOX-MEMO, intrinsics) run as before.

**Every change, whatever its kind:**
- outputs byte-identical to scratch (`soundness.py`, `incr_bench.py --verify`);
- `sweeps.yml` green with BOX-MEMO on, verify and off, on the final full SHA;
- P-T1 unchanged;
- expected data only from the oracle.

## 5. The measurement that would justify each

- **Primary measure: instructions.** They do not move with load: `perf` on the PC runner, the
  PMU on Apple silicon.
- **Cycles** are also measured where the change is about memory (E2).
- **User CPU on GitHub's M1 VMs** is used for E1: the VMs have no PMU, and M1 is §1.2's slowest
  supported Mac.
- **The cold set:**
  - plain-100, full-100 and beamer-30;
  - the beamer-madrid, beamer-polish and framed-tikz-theorems fixtures;
  - *Infinite Descent* (a real book, 592 pages).
- **The keystroke set:** T7's rows and the x2-late `edited_instr_k` (`t7.py`).
- **The bars:**

| experiment | bar |
|---|---|
| E1 | ≥ 5 % CPU on M1-class, on both the cold rows and the keystroke rows |
| E2 | ≥ 1 % instructions or cycles on the cold set, with no row worse |
| E3 | ≥ 10 % of a real deck's cold compile |
| any item | a win beside an unchanged result |

## 6. The ablation

- **E1:** the same SHA built with and without PGO, run interleaved on one machine.
- **E2:** the PR's head against its merge base, an A/B build that differs only in the change.
  Its number stands only if the PDFs are identical.
- **E3:** a 2 × 2:
  - BOX-MEMO off against on, recording anywhere;
  - `\XC@col@rlet` alone against every name (`FLASHTEX_BOXMEMO_NAMES`).

  The macro profile (`FLASHTEX_MACRO_PROFILE`) attributes what remains. A MACRO-REPLAY
  prototype would add a third column, its own replay against none.
- **Where the results go:** every experiment publishes `runs.jsonl` and the summary in
  `docs/evidence/l6-*`, with VERIFIED kept separate from REPORTED.
