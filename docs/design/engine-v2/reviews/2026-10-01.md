## 1. Verdict

**The direction is right; execution, not design, is now the bottleneck.** D1 (a faithful engine)
keeps paying off: the parity fixtures stay at 83/83 P-T1 and P-T2 on tonight's main, the arXiv
failures are still the three known engine bugs, the engine is already faster than C pdfTeX on
cold full compiles (0.58–0.95× on the Mac, REPORTED #1309), and an edit's page costs the engine
3–9 ms p95 (REPORTED, #1269). What isn't working is the path from a finished fix to main and the
gates around it:

- the PRs that fix the only remaining parity failures wait for review or landing;
- CI's merge pass rate is 51 %, almost all of it infrastructure;
- the nightly T2 gate has never completed on main;
- main is red on `typst-host` (a protocol version collision);
- the app's keystroke → commit misses the owner's 16 ms gate until keep-warm (#1300) lands;
- the P5 app-parity lane has not started, and the machine it was given has run out of quota.

No numbered decision (D1–D14) needs to change. Ten adopted refinements and three owner questions
follow (§7).

## 3. What is working (with evidence)

1. **Parity holds and the gate now guards it.** #1262 makes lockstep plus the new engine's
   P-T1/P-T2 fixtures required in `merge_group` (`ci.yml` `ci-required` refuses a merge group in
   which neither `engine-parity` job succeeded; VERIFIED). Tonight on main `a2fed9546`: P-T1 83/83,
   P-T2 83/83 (VERIFIED, §5).
2. **Speed beyond the target.** Cold full compiles, Mac, release, bounds-checked, no PGO: main is
   0.59–0.95× pdfTeX's CPU across 10–1,000 pages (#1309's "before (main)" column, REPORTED); #1309
   saves up to 3 % more. Since 2026-09-30 the engine no longer needs anything unproven
   (unchecked reads stay a benchmark feature, §4.2).
3. **Incremental, engine side** (#1269, Mac, REPORTED): edited page p95 3.1–10.6 ms on all eight
   bench documents; preamble edit 41–281 ms (≤ 400 ms row met); full-1000 convergence 22/66 →
   50/66, pages re-run p50 64 → 10; the owner's 1,072-page book went from re-typesetting 943 pages
   per keystroke to converging within 3–33.
4. **Memory** (#1300, PC, REPORTED): peak RSS while typing plain-1000 7.4 → 0.62 GB, full-1000
   14 GB (killed) → 1.50 GB, book.tex 10 GB (killed) → 1.02 GB.
5. **The soundness sweeps work.** In 24 hours they caught five real bugs before they shipped
   (REPORTED): the `.vrb` barrier (7/8,500), the `pdf_char_used` union (103/8,500), the NUL `.toc`
   (#1294), images after a restore (#1295) and a checkpoint inside a fragile frame (36/50 at a
   0.2 ms checkpoint interval). Each was fixed with a test that fails before the fix.
6. **External tools and multipass** (#1296, #1308, REPORTED): 113/113 documents P-T2-identical to
   `latexmk -pdf`, every `.bbl`/`.ind` byte-identical; a `\cite` edit takes 0.74× latexmk's wall
   (biblatex-120) and 0.72× (book-300); the lane's ≤ 0.5× target is not met yet.
7. **The app.** Reopen from stored pages: 16–29 ms in-app, 111–365 ms at launch (#1332, REPORTED,
   not landed); the v3 pane has forward/reverse search, dark preview and all font kinds on main.

## 4. What isn't working

**F1. Fixes for the remaining parity failures don't reach main.** The arXiv failures are #1218
(PK/Type 3), #1219 (a panic) and #1220 (font expansion) (2026-09-30 track 4). #1280 landed one part
of #1220. #1282, #1279 and #1289 have waited 17–19 h with no current verdict. Daniel's 200-document
T4 slice (#2, 09-30 23:38–23:56Z, REPORTED) puts a number on it: L0–L3 175/175, and once a harness
artefact (EPS conversion dates) is removed, P-T2 175/175 and **P-T1 170/175 on main; all 5 failures
are #1283 (`glue set`), and all 5 pass on #1289's build**. So the stronger P5 gate's P-T1 ≥ 98 %
is 97.1 % on main and 100 % on that slice with one unreviewed PR. #1279's reviewer traced its last
mismatch to the port's hash layout (`hash_prime` 1777 against TeX Live's `tex.ch` 8501,
`hash_size` 15000); the fix is #1285, MERGE-READY at 02:19Z and not queued. Parity is priority 1,
and it is waiting on review and landing, not on engineering.

**F2. The landing rule is ambiguous, so engine PRs stall.** The Commander's ruling of 09-30
17:36Z lets any machine queue its own MERGE-READY PRs, engine paths included, and reserves only
`.github/workflows` and DESIGN.md. The landing lane then marked #1302, #1279 and #1282
"NEEDS-COMMANDER: touches `crates/flashtex-engine/**`" (18:19–18:41Z). #1302 has been MERGE-READY
for 11 h. §9.5 does not state the rule, so each lane reads it differently.

**F3. Stacking has already lost work.** §9.5 says "no stacking". Since the last review #1308 was
stacked on #1296, #1310 on #1308, #1313 on #1296, #1309 merges #1285, #1287 was stacked on #1254.
#1310 merged into #1308's branch after #1308 had landed, so its content never reached main (#1317
re-opens it).

**F4. CI is unreliable, and the failures are infrastructure.** 51 % merge_group pass rate,
success median 56 min (§2). The PC ran out of memory twice (now capped, #1327, open), its runners
are off tonight, Daniel's Mac ran at load ≈ 100 (#2 23:02Z), and hosted macOS `swift test` fails
intermittently. The decision of 2026-09-30 (b) is half-built: **`trip`, `etrip` and
`pdftex-regression` are still not in `CI required`'s needs** (`ci.yml` l.1060–1076, VERIFIED), and
the nightly T2 has never completed on main.

**F5. Main is red, and the cause is a design gap.** #1296 made `display-list-v3` 3.2 mean
external tools (spec l.105, VERIFIED); §15.4 and #1303 use "v3.2" for the Typst extension. The
shared reference client now says `[3, 2]` and `typst-host`'s test expects `[3, 1]`. Path-filtered
Typst CI is outside the required checks (TY8, by design), so the queue did not stop it.

**F6. The owner's latency gate is not met in the app yet.** Decision 1: key event → preview
commit ≤ 16 ms p95. App-v3-5 measured key → commit p50/p95 12.7/21.6 ms (plain-10), 13.3/20.2
(plain-120), 24.5/30.6 (plain-1000), keep-warm off; key → presented adds 16–24 ms of window server,
never below two 120 Hz frames (REPORTED, #1332's evidence). The host's first page is most of it:
Apple Silicon runs an idle core at about a third of its clock, and keep-warm takes the host to
8–15 ms p50 (#1269 §6). Keep-warm (decision 10) is in #1300, which waits on #1313.

**F7. The P5 app-parity lane has not started, and its machine has no quota.** Decision 3 deletes
the old engine once the new one reaches full parity with it. J3 (`P5-APP-PARITY`, the gap list
first) was never started, nor were J1 (zero tolerance, the P3 exit), J2 or J4 (#1319 05:08Z).
Jaysen's plan is at about 90 %.

**F8. Convergence is guarded by hand-proved rules, and two were unsound.** P4-FINISH added
dead-word rules (whatsit words, high-water marks, `intr_state`); the `pdf_char_used` union and the
(b′) read test were unsound and were caught only by the sweeps (§3 item 5). Object numbers are
still not relocatable, and input line numbers are still not shifted (`incr.rs`, VERIFIED): the 09-30
rule (c) is half-built, and **no harness measures newline or paragraph split/join edits**
(`tools/incr-bench/matrix.py` runs letters and sentences only, VERIFIED), although T7 names them.

**F9. Where bulk work pays, and a duplicate.** Lockstep grew 260 → 1,204 cases in a day (all

harness artefact hiding the date line. Real-document breadth and edit replay pay more than more
primitive cases now. #1275 (`tools/latency-bench`) duplicates `tools/incr-bench` against
decision (d).

**F10. Governance drift.** `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` still say DESIGN.md "is re-verified every two
